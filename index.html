About Amanraj Gill (ARG)

Hailing from the heartland of Haryana, **Amanraj Gill (ARG)** has carved an unmistakable legacy as a songwriter, composer, and vocalist whose voice cuts straight to the core of folk ethos and contemporary music.

With an ability to move effortlessly between thunderous desi anthems like Tag Awara Ke, Hathkadiyan, and Circle, to soulful, poignant records like Hopeless and Kasht, ARG continues to set streaming records.

His distinct, unapologetic voice and signature sound have made him one of the most streamed and celebrated regional artists on Spotify and YouTube worldwide.
